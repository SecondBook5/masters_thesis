\chapter{Discussion and Conclusions} \label{chap:discussion}

\section{Principal findings}

This work asked a single, deliberately narrow question: during premalignant epithelial progression,
does the local tissue microenvironment alter the progression-aligned regulatory state of an epithelial
cell beyond what its own intrinsic state already predicts, and if so, through which regulatory programs
and at what spatial scale. StageBridge answered that question with a structured universal differential
equation that separates an intrinsic regulatory-progression field from a niche-gated context field and
reads out their difference as a per-program, conditional context residual, $R_{\text{cond}}$. Three
results follow, and they are best stated together because their tension is the contribution.

First, a context-associated component of progression exists and is reproducible. On the preinvasive
AAH$\rightarrow$AIS edge, twenty-nine regulatory programs carried context residuals whose held-out
confidence intervals excluded zero with sign consistent across all five patient-held-out folds and
across leave-one-donor-out resampling; twenty-eight programs met the same bar on the AIS$\rightarrow$
invasive edge. The stable set was a coordinated axis---p53, WNT, Trail, and AP-1 (FOS) gaining context
weight while interferon (IRF9) and the TGF$\beta$ effector SMAD4 lost it---rather than a scatter of
isolated hits, and it was directionally concordant with independent co-expression analyses of the same
tissue. That a small, sign-consistent, program-resolved residual survives patient-level resampling is
the positive result: tissue context is associated with additional regulatory displacement beyond the
progression predicted from epithelial state alone.

Second, and on the same data, that contextual information did not resolve at the receiver-local scale.
A model given each cell's own local niche had a lower mean endpoint distance than self-only transport
and was favored in four of five folds, but the paired difference did not reach significance ($8.76$
semantic Sinkhorn units, $95\%$ CI $[-2.83, +20.35]$, $P \approx 0.10$), and local context was
statistically equivalent to a stratum-matched shuffle of the same niche features ($0.08$ units,
$[-1.10, +1.26]$). The well-powered result is thus the equivalence to shuffled context, not a
demonstrated advantage over self-only: cell-resolved niche neither clears self-only with significance
nor separates from a marginal-structure-preserving shuffle. A variance
decomposition of the local composition features supplied the reason---$85$ to $96\%$ of the variance in
macrophage, fibroblast, and CAF abundance was receiver-local and stage-invariant, leaving little
stage-aligned signal for a cell-resolved context feature to carry at the resolution of the spatial
assay. The reproducible contextual information is therefore specimen-scale, not uniquely receiver-local,
in these cohorts.

Third, the estimator behaved correctly where it should have failed. On the four-donor PanIN series the
powered stability filter returned zero stable programs---the correct null at that cohort size---while
the model still recovered a directionally coherent inflammatory-to-proliferative shift. The
cross-disease probe therefore calibrated rather than validated: it demonstrated that StageBridge does
not manufacture patient-robust stability where the data cannot support it.

The scientific shape of the thesis is thus a bounded positive result. Context-associated regulatory
transport is real, interpretable, and reproducible at the program level; its spatial resolution is
bounded to the specimen ecological scale by the available measurements; and the method reports that
boundary rather than obscuring it.

\section{A context-associated component of premalignant progression}

The positive finding is best read through the host-versus-intrinsic framing that motivated the
design. An epithelial cell does not progress in isolation; it progresses within a tissue whose
inflammatory and stromal composition is itself changing across the precursor sequence
\autocite{Kadara2016-dt,Mazzilli2025-hi}. StageBridge operationalizes that intuition by refusing to
attribute all of a cell's progression-aligned displacement to its own state: the context field can
only claim the residual displacement that the intrinsic field failed to explain for ecologically
comparable cells.

\subsection{Restraint and drive are co-activated, and the niche grades the balance}

The preinvasive stable set is initially counterintuitive. p53 rises ($+0.295$) alongside WNT
($+0.293$), the AP-1 factor FOS ($+0.141$), and directionally-stable EGFR ($+0.308$), while the
TGF$\beta$ effector SMAD4 falls ($-0.099$). Read as a statement about tumour suppression, coordinated
gain of p53 and WNT would be contradictory. Two considerations resolve it, and the second is the
more interesting.

The first is technical. The PROGENy p53 coordinate is a transcriptional \emph{response footprint}: it
scores the downstream consequence of p53-pathway activation, not p53 function, dosage, or mutational
status \autocite{Schubert2018-ph}. Genomic studies of the LUAD precursor sequence place the
drastic molecular changes---copy-number alteration, global hypomethylation---at the later AIS stage
rather than at its onset, and note that these coincide with the point at which lesional cells become
exposed to the surrounding microenvironment \autocite{Haga2023-xc}; \emph{TP53} mutation is
correspondingly a feature of more advanced disease, where mutant cells lose alveolar identity and
adopt proliferative, high-entropy expression programs \autocite{Zhao2025-vm}. An elevated p53
footprint at AAH$\rightarrow$AIS is therefore what oncogene-induced stress in a lesion with largely
intact restraint machinery should look like.

The second consideration is that this configuration has been observed directly, in an independent
disease, with genetic manipulation available. In mouse models of pancreatic carcinogenesis capturing
spontaneous p53 loss, oncogenic and tumour-suppressive programs---specifically those controlled by
p53, CDKN2A, and SMAD4---are \emph{co-activated} within a discrete progenitor-like population at the
benign-to-malignant transition, engaging senescence-like responses \autocite{Reyes2026-yr}. The
signature reported here is the same configuration read out as a context residual in a different
epithelium: restraint and drive elevated together, with SMAD4 the component that gives way. That two
systems, two species, and two entirely different inference strategies converge on co-activated
suppression and proliferation at the precursor-to-invasion boundary is stronger support than either
provides alone, and it argues that the preinvasive lesion is better described as a contested state
than as a partially transformed one.

The niche-stratum gradient then supplies a direction to that contest. Across the three
conditional-stratified strata, p53 redirection \emph{declines} from $+0.363$ to $+0.240$ while WNT
rises ($+0.275 \rightarrow +0.311$) and TRAIL rises ($+0.182 \rightarrow +0.220$) toward the CAF- and
immune-dense stratum, each significant against a within-donor permutation null. The same axis is
causal in the pancreatic model: p53 suppression enables progenitor-cell expansion, epithelial--
mesenchymal reprogramming, and the formation of an immune-privileged niche \autocite{Reyes2026-yr}.
The observation here is correlative and its magnitude is modest, but its direction is the one that
system predicts---the inflamed, stroma-rich end of the ecological gradient is where restraint is
weakest and drive is strongest within the same lesion class.

That heterogeneity within a histologic class is precisely what the estimand was built to expose.
Molecular archetype analysis of LUAD premalignant lesions, performed without regard to histologic
designation, resolves lesions into groups that differ in proliferation, immune environment, and
driver enrichment; the ``proliferation'' archetype is enriched for EGFR driver mutations and a
pro-tumour immune environment, and tumours closest to it show significantly lower disease-free
survival, while a ``normal-like'' archetype carries an anti-tumour immune response and no driver
enrichment \autocite{Anderson2026-zg}. EGFR is the largest directionally-stable context effect at the
preinvasive edge in this work. The convergence is suggestive rather than confirmatory---archetypes
are bulk-defined and this analysis is receiver-level---but both point to the same conclusion: AAH and
AIS are not one biological entity, and the axis on which they differ has an immune-ecological
component.

\subsection{Interferon tone is lost before invasion, not after it}

The most striking model-independent result is a coordinated reversal. Interferon-$\alpha$ and
interferon-$\gamma$ responses, oxidative phosphorylation, and MYC targets are all downregulated at
AAH$\rightarrow$AIS and all upregulated at AIS$\rightarrow$invasive. The context-residual estimand
agrees at the preinvasive step through two independent coordinates: JAK-STAT is directionally
downregulated ($-0.246$) and IRF9---a component of the ISGF3 complex that executes canonical type-I
interferon transcription---is stably downregulated ($-0.129$). Two analytic lenses, one
whole-transcriptome and one model-based, place interferon suppression at the \emph{preinvasive}
transition.

This matters because the only human cohort in which premalignant lesions have been molecularly
profiled \emph{and} followed to a known fate reports the same direction with an outcome attached. In
longitudinally monitored bronchial premalignant lesions, progressive and persistent lesions of the
proliferative subtype show decreased interferon signalling and antigen processing/presentation
relative to regressive lesions, with immunofluorescence confirming depletion of innate and adaptive
immune cells \autocite{Beane2019-mp}. Those are squamous airway precursors rather than
adenocarcinoma precursors, so the comparison is across lesion types and must be made carefully. But
the claim it licenses is specific: loss of interferon tone in a premalignant lesion is associated
with progression rather than regression, and the present analysis places that loss at the
preinvasive step of the adenocarcinoma sequence.

Adjacent evidence in LUAD is consistent. Immunosurveillance is suppressed across the early
adenocarcinoma spectrum, with reduced cytolytic natural-killer signatures and increased regulatory
T cells already apparent in subsolid nodules and premalignant lesions
\autocite{Yanagawa2023-vg,Sinjab2021-in}. In the primary cohort analysed here, epithelial--
proinflammatory niches enriched for IL1B-high macrophages are prevalent in precursor lesions and
become \emph{less} frequent in established LUAD \autocite{Peng2025-xu}. The pancreatic literature
describes the same temporal ordering from the opposite direction: lymphoid organization adjacent to
PanIN is immature relative to PDAC, and immune exclusion and immunosuppression emerge during
progression rather than being present at the precursor stage \autocite{Lyman2025-qg}, with focal
immunosuppressive hotspots enriched for regulatory T cells and macrophages appearing around
higher-grade dysplasia \autocite{Kiemen2026-dz}.

The synthesis is that the immune configuration of a precursor lesion is a stage-specific property
with a limited window, and the results here locate the transcriptional disengagement from interferon
surveillance before invasion rather than after it. That has a direct interception implication: in the
same cohort, targeting inflammation alone or with checkpoint blockade during the precancerous phase
reduces alveolar progenitor burden \autocite{Peng2025-xu}, and in pancreas, pharmacological
interception of established precursors confers a greater survival benefit than treatment of
established tumours \autocite{Than2026-hj}. If the interferon window closes preinvasively, the
interval in which immune-directed interception is most likely to matter is earlier than the invasive
transition that current staging emphasises \autocite{Mazzilli2025-hi,Pedro2025-kz}.

The competing explanation must be stated with equal weight. The invasive front carries a different
cellular composition, with documented stage-associated shifts in regulatory T cells, B-cell subsets,
and endothelium across the AIS$\rightarrow$MIA$\rightarrow$invasive sequence
\autocite{Zhu2022-hz}; although receiver states are epithelial-decoded, residual composition effects
cannot be excluded from a cross-sectional design. A restored interferon signature at invasion is as
consistent with immune infiltration as with epithelial re-engagement. The preinvasive loss is the
more defensible half of the observation, and it is the half that carries the interception
implication.

\subsection{Lineage identity is retained preinvasively and dismantled at invasion}

The enrichment results place loss of differentiated identity at the invasion boundary rather than
before it. Ciliary and axonemal programs are \emph{up} at AAH$\rightarrow$AIS (axoneme assembly, NES
$+2.16$; cilium movement, $+2.15$) and collapse at AIS$\rightarrow$invasive, where sensory perception
of chemical stimulus is the strongest single enrichment on either edge (NES $-2.61$). The regulatory
readout nominates concordant factors at invasion: the pioneer and endodermal lineage factor FOXA1
gains context weight ($+0.192$) and the Notch coactivator MAML1 is the largest stable
transcription-factor effect ($+0.292$)---a lineage-specification program being actively rewritten
rather than passively lost.

This staging is consistent with functional work on plasticity in lung tumourigenesis. A
high-plasticity cell state gives rise to both differentiated and progressed cancer states, and its
ablation in early neoplasias abrogates the benign-to-malignant transition in mouse autochthonous
lung tumours \autocite{Chan2026-ov}; spatial profiling of human LUAD subtypes similarly resolves
heterogeneous dedifferentiation states across histologic categories \autocite{Wang2023-hg}, and loss
of alveolar identity is a defining property of \emph{TP53}-mutant malignant cells
\autocite{Zhao2025-vm}. The reading supported here is that preinvasive lesions in this cohort expand
while retaining a differentiated airway-like transcriptional identity, and that the dismantling of
that identity is concentrated at the invasive step. The regulatory basis used in this work cannot
resolve alveolar-intermediate or high-plasticity states directly, so this is offered as a
directional concordance rather than as identification of those populations.

The metabolic half of the reversal has a cross-disease parallel worth noting. Deep visual proteomics
of pancreatic precursors finds metabolic reprogramming initiating in histologically normal ducts and
intensifying across PanIN progression, with mitochondrial remodelling becoming prominent in
high-grade PanIN \emph{before} invasion \autocite{Min2026-lo}. The oxidative-phosphorylation
suppression observed here at the preinvasive step and its restoration at invasion describes a
different trajectory in a different tissue, but both analyses place metabolic reorganization among
the earliest events and before histologic transformation is complete.

\subsection{Reading a context residual is not reading a level}

One result requires an explicit interpretive guard, because the naive reading contradicts
well-established tumour biology. VEGF is the largest-magnitude stable pathway at the invasion
boundary and it is redirected \emph{downward} ($-0.318$). Invasion is canonically accompanied by
angiogenesis, so a negative VEGF term invites the objection that the model has recovered the wrong
sign.

Two features of the estimand dissolve the apparent conflict. First, $R_{\mathrm{cond}}$ is a
\emph{residual}, not a level: it reports how the surrounding tissue redirects a receiver's
progression relative to the trajectory predicted from its own state, and carries no claim about the
absolute abundance of VEGF signalling at either stage. A pathway can rise across a transition while
its context-attributable component is negative. Second, the receiver is epithelial, and the PROGENy
VEGF coordinate scores the transcriptional response to VEGF signalling in the cells being scored.
Angiogenic VEGF signalling is transduced predominantly by endothelium, so epithelial VEGF-response
redirection and tissue-level angiogenesis are distinct quantities that need not move together.

Independent evidence places the angiogenic contribution in the compartment the estimand excludes.
Multiomic profiling of the AAH$\rightarrow$AIS$\rightarrow$MIA sequence in never-smokers finds that
endothelial and stromal activity, not epithelial state, distinguishes molecular subtypes within
pathologically identical lesions, and identifies angiogenesis in late-stage AIS as a probable
contributor to tissue invasion at the MIA stage \autocite{Chen2025-zo}. The co-expression analysis in
this work agrees: the vascular SM4 module is enriched for regulation of cell migration (padj
$=0.007$), TGF$\beta$ response, MAPK cascade, and angiogenesis regulation, and the stromal SM6 ECM
module gains significance specifically at invasion (supramolecular fibre organization, padj $=0.052$;
leading-edge COL1A1, COL3A1, LTBP2, COL5A2). The tissue-level vascular and matrix-remodelling
programs are active at invasion in the same data in which the epithelial VEGF context residual is
negative. The consistent reading is that the invasive niche redirects epithelial regulatory state
away from a VEGF-response program while the vascular and stromal compartments remodel---a
compartment-resolved statement that a level-based analysis could not make, and one the estimand's
construction is specifically designed to express.

The same guard applies to SMAD4. Its context downregulation at the preinvasive step ($-0.099$) is a
TGF$\beta$-effector response footprint and is not evidence of the \emph{SMAD4} deletion that
characterizes late pancreatic carcinogenesis, although the convergence with the SMAD4 arm of the
co-activated suppressive program in pancreatic precursors \autocite{Reyes2026-yr} and with
TGF$\beta$-dependent tumour--microenvironment signalling at LUAD invasion \autocite{Zhu2022-hz} is
worth noting given the cross-disease design.

\subsection{The gated communication chains recover the return arm of a known circuit}

The $R_{\mathrm{cond}}$-gated communication analysis narrows roughly $1.3$ million candidate
sender$\rightarrow$ligand$\rightarrow$receptor$\rightarrow$program chains at the preinvasive edge onto
two programs, FOS and RB1, with macrophage- and stromal-derived APOE, GRN, PSAP, and A2M routed
through LRP1 and EGFR. Because the gate is a filter rather than an independent measurement, the
relevant question is not whether it is selective---it is selective by construction---but whether what
survives is biologically coherent. Three observations suggest it is.

First, the retained ligands converge on receptors that plausibly bind them: APOE, A2M, and PSAP are
established LRP1 ligands.\footnote{The best-characterized receptor for GRN is sortilin; LRP1
engagement is reported but weaker, so this claim is restricted to APOE, A2M, and PSAP.} A gate
operating on the terminal regulatory program, with no knowledge of receptor pharmacology, recovered a
ligand set sharing a receptor. Second, the EGFR$\rightarrow$FOS chains reproduce the canonical
growth-factor to immediate-early-gene axis, in which receptor tyrosine kinase signalling induces AP-1
components through the MAPK cascade. That the gate recovers textbook wiring is not a discovery, but a
filter returning noise would not be expected to reconstitute a well-characterized signalling axis and
a shared-receptor ligand family from a million-candidate pool.

Third, and most substantively, the chains nominate the reciprocal arm of a circuit shown to be
required for tumour initiation. In mouse lung, KRAS-mutant alveolar type II cells adopt
regenerative-like states and secrete amphiregulin, activating EGFR signalling in adjacent
fibroblasts and inducing a fibrotic, injury-like program; reprogrammed fibroblasts then expand and
reprogram alveolar macrophages, amplifying inflammatory signalling and reinforcing epithelial
plasticity, and disruption of the amphiregulin--EGFR axis prevents niche formation and abrogates
tumour initiation \autocite{Cardoso2026-kp}. That circuit describes signal flowing \emph{from}
epithelium \emph{to} stroma and myeloid cells. The chains recovered here describe the return path:
macrophage- and stromal-derived ligands signalling back onto epithelial EGFR and LRP1 and terminating
on a context-driven AP-1 program. EGFR appears on both arms. The gate was given no knowledge of that
circuit, and the two analyses together close a loop that neither describes completely---epithelial
initiation of a fibrotic-inflammatory niche, and that niche redirecting the regulatory state of the
epithelium that built it.

The chains remain nominations. Ligand--receptor inference and the gate read the same expression
matrix, no interaction is measured directly, and the specific senders identified here (APOE, GRN,
PSAP, A2M from alveolar and CHIT1$^{+}$ macrophages; LTF, C3, PLA2G2A from mesothelial and
serous-gland cells) are hypotheses for perturbation in organoid or co-culture systems---the same
systems in which the forward arm was established \autocite{Cardoso2026-kp}. Their value is that they
are specific enough to be wrong.

\section{The spatial scale of ecological information is the central inferential result}
\label{sec:scale}

The most consequential result is the negative one, and it is a contribution rather than a caveat. The
question of whether the microenvironmental information relevant to progression is resolved at the scale
of a single cell's neighborhood, or only at the coarser scale of a specimen's overall ecology, is
rarely posed sharply and almost never answered with a pre-registered falsification test. Here it was
posed and answered: cell-resolved local context did not outperform specimen-level ecology or a matched
shuffle. This does not license the conclusion that local biology is unimportant. It licenses a narrower
and more useful statement---that the available spatial measurements did not provide stable incremental
information at the receiver-local scale beyond what broad specimen ecology already carried.

The variance decomposition explains why, and in doing so converts the negative into a measurement
prescription. If $85$ to $96\%$ of the variation in local niche composition is receiver-local and
stage-invariant, then a cell-resolved context feature is dominated by variation unrelated to
progression, and no estimator can recover from that feature what the data did not encode in it.
Detecting a receiver-local effect, if one exists, would require spatial data in which niche composition
varies with progression stage---higher-resolution assays, more shared donors per edge than the two
available on the primary edge, or both. This is the sense in which the thesis defines the boundary
between what indirect observation can currently recover and what must still be measured.

\subsection{Why the receiver-local scale failed, and why that may be biology}

The falsification result---cell-resolved niche context statistically equivalent to a
stratum-matched shuffle, with $85$ to $96\%$ of variance in local composition features receiver-local
and stage-invariant---was presented above as a measurement boundary. Two independent lines of
evidence suggest it may be more than that.

The first is a direct measurement of the relevant length scale. Three-dimensional
cellular-resolution histology of more than a thousand human pancreatic precancers finds that
inflammation around individual lesions is highly heterogeneous, with immune hotspots and cold spots
\emph{interchanging over tens of microns} \autocite{Kiemen2026-dz}. Visium spots are $55\,\mu$m in
diameter at $100\,\mu$m centres. An imaging modality with no relationship to this analysis therefore
measures the ecological variation as operating at or below the resolution at which the context
vector here was constructed. The variance decomposition reported in Chapter~\ref{chap:results} is a
statistical inference of the same fact from transcriptomic data: most of the ecological variation a
Visium spot can see is variation the spot cannot resolve.

The second is that the progression-relevant stromal state may not be a per-receiver property at all.
Spatial mapping of the PanIN-to-PDAC transition in donor organs finds that the epithelial component
of PanIN lies on a continuum with cancer while the PanIN microenvironment is drastically distinct
from it, with epithelial and stromal compartments evolving asynchronously---and proposes that the
absence of stromal reprogramming is what explains why most PanINs never progress
\autocite{Elhossiny2026-pq}. If the gating variable is whether a specimen's stroma has undergone a
field-level reprogramming, then it is a property of the tissue and not of any individual receiver's
neighbourhood, and a receiver-local context feature would be measuring the wrong object however well
it was resolved. The observation that a cancer-specific fibroblast population defined in that work
carries the WNT component LEF1 \autocite{Elhossiny2026-pq} is a small point of contact with the
sustained WNT context effect reported here across both LUAD edges, though the compartments differ and
no more should be read into it.

These two readings are not exclusive, and the present data cannot separate them. Ecological
information may be receiver-local but below Visium resolution, in which case higher-resolution assays
would recover it; or it may be genuinely specimen-scale, in which case the correct estimand is a
field-level one and additional resolution would not help. The falsification result is compatible with
both, and distinguishing them---by re-running the identical ladder on single-cell-resolution spatial
data, where the first hypothesis predicts recovery and the second does not---is the sharpest single
experiment this thesis defines.

\subsection{The preinvasive field is closer to equilibrium than the invasive field}

A final dynamical observation connects the regulatory results to the clinical asymmetry that
motivates interception. The Helmholtz decomposition of the fitted field partitions velocity into
gradient (equilibrium) and rotational (non-equilibrium) components; the rotational fraction is
$0.48$ on the preinvasive edge and $0.77$ at invasion, and adding context changes it by at most
$0.011$ on any edge. The invasive transition is the strongly non-gradient one---a directed,
cycle-like flux rather than relaxation toward a fixed point---while the preinvasive transition
retains substantially more gradient character.

That difference has a natural reading in a setting where precursor burden is enormous and progression
is rare. The normal adult pancreas harbours hundreds of PanINs, almost all bearing oncogenic KRAS
hotspot mutations, the great majority of which never become cancer
\autocite{Braxton2024-sv,Kiemen2024-xk}; in the airway, carcinoma in situ lesions that are
microscopically identical are in genuine equipoise, with roughly half progressing and half regressing
or remaining static \autocite{Teixeira2019-yb}. A transition with a large gradient component behaves
like movement within a basin, from which return or stasis is available; a strongly rotational field
describes a flow that does not settle. The preinvasive step in these cohorts looks more like the
former and the invasive step more like the latter, which is the shape the epidemiology of precursor
lesions would predict. The convergence with the description of \emph{TP53}-mutant malignant cells as
occupying high-entropy expression programs \autocite{Zhao2025-vm} is suggestive of a shared
underlying transition in disorder, though the two quantities are not the same measurement.

This is a property of the learned field rather than an observed dynamical quantity, and the
near-invariance of the rotational fraction to context locates it in the intrinsic progression field
rather than in the niche-gated term. It describes the geometry the model inferred from stage-ordered
snapshots, not a measured energy landscape, and it is offered as an interpretive frame for why
preinvasive lesions are so often indolent rather than as evidence that they are.

\section{Methodological contribution}

The methodological contribution is threefold and is largely independent of the specific biological
result. First, the estimand: $R_{\text{cond}}$ is a per-program, conditional, spatially mappable
contrast that isolates the context-attributable component of regulatory transport within ecologically
comparable populations, formalized through conditional Monge maps \autocite{Bunne2022-go} rather than
left as an implicit property of a black-box predictor. Second, the identifiability discipline: the
coupling used to define transport was selected against a frozen synthetic benchmark with four
pre-specified acceptance bars, and that benchmark falsified the first candidate design---the strict
state-neutral coupling passed abstract-latent recovery but failed regulatory-space recovery, which
motivated the conditional-stratified revision. Reporting a benchmark that changed the method, rather
than a benchmark constructed to endorse it, is a deliberate stance against confirmation. Third, the
falsification ladder: rather than presenting a single fitted model, the analysis compared it against
self-only, specimen-average, shuffled-context, and degenerate single-branch alternatives, so that the
claim attached to the model is exactly the claim the comparisons support---no more.

Two honest limits of the decomposition were reported rather than hidden. The credibility diagnostic
$D_{\rho}$ showed that the interpretable structured terms carry velocity of magnitude comparable to the
neural residuals in the fitted field (mean $0.965$, credible in four of five folds), so the per-program
attribution is interpreted only to that extent. And an unstructured neural field fit the endpoint
better than the structured decomposition---the expected and accepted cost of trading raw predictive fit
for an interpretable, program-resolved field. These are the prices of interpretability, and stating them
is part of the method's claim to trustworthiness.

\section{Limitations}

Several limitations bound the interpretation. The progression coordinate $\tau$ is normalized
pathological stage, not clock time; the model captures the direction and magnitude of state change
aligned with progression, not growth rates or real-time dynamics. The receiver states are
DestVI-decoded epithelial expression from mixed-cell Visium spots, not dissociated single nuclei, and
although adequacy gates validated that the decoded states recapitulate study-defined precursor biology,
they remain inferred quantities. The context residual is a conditional transport contrast, identifiable
given the coarse niche stratum; it is not an individual causal effect and does not recover historical
cell lineages. The primary edge carried only two donors contributing epithelium to both stages, and the
invasion boundary carried none at the AIS$\rightarrow$MIA step, which limits the within-patient contrast
available to distinguish local from specimen-level context and is the proximate reason the
receiver-local hypothesis could not be resolved. The intrinsic field is fitted on source--target pairs
that themselves contain ecological structure, so $R_{\text{cond}}$ is a conservative estimate under the
fitted decomposition rather than a formal lower bound on the true ecological effect. Finally, the
co-expression and communication analyses read the same expression matrix as the estimand, so their
agreement with $R_{\text{cond}}$ is internal cross-method consistency, not independent validation, and
the communication chains are mechanistic nominations for experimental follow-up rather than established
interactions.

\section{Future directions}

The results point to specific next experiments rather than open-ended extensions. The most direct is a
retrained-shuffle null: retraining the full pipeline on stratum-permuted context and recounting stable
programs would establish whether the twenty-nine stable programs exceed a shuffled-context baseline at
the estimand level, complementing the endpoint-level shuffle already reported. Resolving the spatial
scale of ecological information---the boundary this thesis defined but could not cross---requires
spatial data in which niche composition varies with stage: single-cell-resolution spatial assays, or
cohorts with more shared donors per progression edge. The communication framework generated concrete,
testable hypotheses (macrophage- and stromal-derived APOE, GRN, and DCN signaling routed through LRP1
and EGFR onto the context-driven FOS and RB1 programs) that are candidates for perturbation in organoid
or co-culture models. And the identifiability framework itself is edge- and disease-agnostic; applying
it to other premalignant sequences with adequate shared-donor support would test whether the
specimen-scale character of ecological information is specific to these lung and pancreatic cohorts or a
general feature of premalignant progression at current spatial resolution.

\section{Conclusion}

StageBridge reconstructed progression-associated regulatory transport across held-out patients in the
premalignant lung and identified a reproducible, interpretable subset of regulatory programs associated
with tissue context beyond epithelial receiver state. It then subjected that finding to a pre-registered
falsification and reported the result honestly: receiver-local context did not consistently outperform
specimen-level ecology or matched shuffled controls, and the ecological variation that would carry a
local effect is overwhelmingly specimen-scale and stage-invariant at the resolution of the available
data. The pancreatic series confirmed that the estimator calibrates to the correct null when a cohort
cannot support patient-robust stability. The work supports a context-associated component of
premalignant epithelial progression while defining the unresolved spatial scale of that
association---delivering not an unqualified positive claim, but a prespecified estimand with a
characterized recovery boundary, which is the more durable contribution.
